SeeNMeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeeNMeet ("we," "our," or "the app"), developed by Done Deal Entertainment LLC, collects, uses, and protects your information. By using SeeNMeet, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Profile information — name, age, gender, bio, occupation, and other details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Photos and videos — profile photos and video messages you upload or record within the app.
  • Location — general location (city/state/country) to help match you with nearby users.
  • Messages — text, photo, and video messages sent between users through the app's chat feature.
  • Account and device information — email address, device identifiers, and app usage data collected automatically.
  • Purchase information — subscription and purchase status, handled through Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store payment card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your profile and match you with other users.
  • To enable messaging, video calling, and in-app games between users.
  • To send notifications about matches, messages, and app activity.
  • To moderate content and enforce community guidelines, including automated review of uploaded photos and videos.
  • To provide customer support and respond to reports or safety concerns.
  • To improve app performance and features through aggregated usage analytics.
  • To process subscription purchases and manage premium features.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Other users, limited to what you choose to display on your profile and what you send directly through messages.
  • Service providers that support app functionality, including Firebase (authentication, storage, database, messaging), Google AdMob (advertising), and RevenueCat (subscription management).
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where required by law or to protect user safety.

Advertising

SeeNMeet displays ads through Google AdMob to support free features. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve relevant ads. You can review Google's advertising practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, messages, and uploaded media are removed from active systems within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or safety purposes.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete your profile information at any time within the app.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting us at the email below.
  • You can block or report other users directly within the app.
  • You can manage notification preferences through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

SeeNMeet is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will take steps to remove the account and associated data.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including Firebase's security infrastructure, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
