Delete Your SeeNMeet Account

Last updated: September 15, 2026

SeeNMeet, developed by Done Deal Entertainment LLC, lets you delete your account and associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account In-App

  1. Open the SeeNMeet app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Account, then Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Request Deletion Without In-App Access

If you're unable to log in to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at issac@donedealstudios.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "Delete My Account" in the subject line. We'll verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, photos, bio, preferences)
  • Your messages and conversation history
  • Your account credentials and login information
  • Your matches and activity history

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited data for a period after deletion where required for legal, safety, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes, including:

  • Records of Terms of Service violations or safety reports, retained for up to 90 days
  • Transaction and subscription records required for financial/tax compliance, retained as required by law
  • Aggregated, de-identified analytics data that cannot be linked back to you

All other personal data is permanently deleted from our active systems within 30 days of your request.
