Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

Triple Threat Blackjack stores gameplay data locally on your device, and syncs it to secure cloud storage (Google Firebase) if you sign in with a Google or Apple account. Guest players' data stays local to their device only. We do not collect your email, phone number, or other personally identifiable information beyond what your sign-in provider shares with us.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to save and sync your progress across sessions and devices, display your stats and achievements, and enable the friend referral feature. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
