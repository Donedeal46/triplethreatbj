SeeNMeet Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 19, 2026

SeeNMeet, operated by Done Deal Entertainment LLC, strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We do not permit minors to use this App — all users must confirm they are 18 years of age or older to create an account, and accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are terminated.

We use automated content moderation on all uploaded photos and videos to detect and remove violating content. Any account found to violate this policy is immediately banned and, where applicable, reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities.

Users can report any content or account that concerns them directly within the App using the built-in Report feature, or by contacting us at issaccrawford@hotmail.com.
